Lesson 02 · toward fingerstyle

Clean Fingers — Every String Rings

You held 60 BPM. Now let's make every note sound clear, even when you dig in.

Today's win: play each chord one string at a time and get every string to ring clean — no buzz, no dead notes. You'll pinpoint exactly which finger is leaning over, and fix it.
Myth, busted: "My fingers are too big/chubby." Almost never true — plenty of large-handed players play spotless. Buzzing comes from fretting with the flat pad of the finger. The cure is the tip. It's technique, not anatomy.

The three fixes

☝️
Fret on your fingertips. Curl each finger so it drops straight down like a little hammer, contacting on the tip near the nail. The tip is narrow, so it won't flop onto the next string.
✋
Thumb in the middle of the neck's back. Not wrapped over the top. A thumb behind the neck automatically lifts your knuckles and puts fingers on their tips.
📏
Press just behind the fret wire. Not in the middle of the fret space. You need far less pressure there, so your hand stays relaxed and fingers don't sprawl.
And ease off the pluck. You don't need to pick hard. In fingerstyle, volume comes from a relaxed, controlled pull toward your palm — not force. Digging in is what shifts your fretting hand in the first place.

The diagnostic checker

Pick a chord. Hold it on the guitar. Then pluck one string at a time, slowly. For each string, tap Clean or Buzz/dead. The checker points you straight at the finger to fix.

Pick a chord to begin.

Practice routine (about 15 min)

1Set the hand (2 min). Thumb behind neck, fingers curled. Form Em — the easiest — and check all 6 strings ring.
2Hunt the buzz (8 min). Do C, then G, then D in the checker. For any buzz: lift that finger, re-place it on the very tip, closer to the fret wire. Re-test.
3Gentle pluck (3 min). Play each chord with a soft p-i-m-a from Lesson 01. Notice: softer pluck = fingers stay put = cleaner sound.
4The "chubby finger" reframe (2 min). Consciously roll onto your fingertips. Feel how a big finger still fits when it stands tall instead of lying flat.
