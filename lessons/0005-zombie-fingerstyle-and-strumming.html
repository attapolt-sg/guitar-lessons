Lesson 05 · your first real song

"Zombie" — Fingerstyle & Strumming

One four-chord loop. Two ways to play it. Zero new chords to learn.

Today's win: play The Cranberries' "Zombie" around its endless loop of Em → C → G → D — first as a steady fingerpick (your rhythm cure), then as a driving strum. Same four shapes you already own.

The whole song is one loop

Here's the secret that makes "Zombie" perfect for you: nearly the entire song — intro, verse, chorus — cycles through the same four chords, one bar each:

Em→ C→ G→ D↺

Count "1 2 3 4" on each chord, then move to the next. Four bars = one full loop, then it repeats. (The original recording dresses these up with jazzy voicings, but the four base shapes are exactly the ones you know.)

Why fingerstyle first? A fixed finger order (p-i-m-a) gives you a built-in metronome — it forces the steady pulse that free strumming lets you lose. Nail the loop slowly with your fingers, and your strumming gets steadier for free.

Mode 1 — Fingerstyle pattern

One bar per chord, eight plucks in "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &":

Pattern: p i m a · p i m a  — thumb on the bass string, then index-middle-ring climb the top three strings, twice per bar.
  • p (thumb) plays the chord's root bass: Em → 6th string, C → 5th, G → 6th, D → 4th. The player shows which one under the diagram.
  • i · m · a always pluck strings 3 · 2 · 1 (G-B-high E), same as your first pattern.
  • Keep the plucks even. Even & slow beats fast & lumpy every time.

Mode 2 — Strumming pattern

Start with the simplest thing that keeps time: one down-strum per beat (all downstrokes). When that's locked, graduate to the classic pop pattern:

Starter: D D D D (down on 1-2-3-4).
Next level: D · D U · U D U — "down, down-up, up-down-up." The player lights arrows so you can see the up-strokes land off the beat.

↓ = brush down toward the floor · ↑ = brush up. Loosen your wrist; let the pick skim the strings, don't dig.

Practice routine (about 20 min)

1Loop the chords silently (4 min). No picking. Just change Em→C→G→D on a slow count, one bar each. Land every change on beat 1.
2Fingerstyle at 50 BPM (7 min). Turn on the player, Fingerstyle mode. Follow the plucks. Goal: one clean loop with no gaps at the chord changes.
3Strum, all-downs (4 min). Switch to Strumming, starter pattern. Same loop, steady downstrokes on every beat.
4Add the up-strokes (5 min). Move to the D · D U · U D U pattern. Slow it right down if the up-strokes trip you.
