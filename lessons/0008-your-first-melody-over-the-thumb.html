Lesson 08 · melody + accompaniment

Your First Melody Over the Thumb

Two things at once, finally: the thumb keeps time underneath while your fingers sing a tune on top.

Today's win: play a short melody on the top strings while your thumb keeps its steady beat — a real fingerstyle texture, on the same Em → C → G → D loop and the same chord shapes you own.

The idea that makes this easy

The melody hides inside the chords you already hold. You don't fret anything new. While you hold each chord, the melody is simply which top string you pluck on each beat. So your fretting hand keeps doing the one job it knows — hold the shape — and the tune comes from your picking-hand choices.

Under it all, the thumb still plays a bass note on every beat — the pulse from Lesson 07, unchanged. The new part is that on beats 1 and 3 a finger plucks a chosen melody note together with the thumb (a pinch), and those notes in a row make the melody.

The melody

One note per pinch, two per bar (on beats 1 and 3). Read left to right, it's a simple rising-and-falling line:

Em: E · B | C: E · C | G: B · G | D: F♯ · D
How each note is made (no new fretting — all inside the chord shape):
• string 1 (high E) plucked with a (ring)   • string 2 (B) plucked with m (middle)
The player names the melody note and which string it's on, every beat.

The player

Blue = the melody note your finger plucks; orange p = the thumb bass. On beats 1 & 3 they land together (a pinch). Beats 2 & 4 are thumb alone. Start slow — 52 BPM — and use the toggle to drop the melody first if you want to warm up the thumb.

Practice routine (about 20 min)

1Thumb alone (4 min). Uncheck "play the melody." Just the thumb bass on every beat around the loop, steady — your Lesson 07 groove, as a warm-up.
2Melody alone, no thumb (5 min). Ignore the player's thumb for a moment. Hold each chord and just pluck the melody notes on beats 1 & 3: E B · E C · B G · F♯ D. Hear the tune on its own.
3Put them together, slowly (7 min). Re-check the melody. On beats 1 & 3, thumb and finger pluck at the same instant (the pinch). Go painfully slow — both notes landing together matters more than speed.
4One clean loop (4 min). Play the full 4 bars once through with melody + thumb, no stops. That's the win.
