The right hand: p · i · m · a

Fingerstyle names each plucking finger with one letter, from the Spanish words. This notation is universal — you'll see it in every fingerstyle book.

LetterSpanishFingerUsual job
ppulgarThumbBass strings — 6th, 5th, 4th (the thick ones)
iíndiceIndex3rd string (G)
mmedioMiddle2nd string (B)
aanularRing1st string (high E, thinnest)

Source: Wikipedia — Fingerstyle guitar. The thumb covers the low strings; index/middle/ring cover the top three treble strings.

String numbering (easy to forget): String 1 is the thinnest (high E) at the floor; string 6 is the thickest (low E) at the ceiling. Higher number = thicker = lower pitch.

Resting-hand shape

  • Thumb (p) sits ahead of the fingers, pointing slightly left, so it doesn't collide with i/m/a.
  • Let the fingers hang relaxed over strings 3-2-1. Pluck by drawing the fingertip toward your palm, not yanking upward.
  • Keep the wrist gently arched, not flat on the soundboard.
